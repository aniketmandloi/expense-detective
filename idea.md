# Expense management software that automatically flags suspicious spending for remote teams

Remote teams are hemorrhaging money on questionable expenses while finance teams drown in manual receipt reviews. Expense Detective uses computer vision and behavioral analysis to automatically flag suspicious spending patterns before they hit your books. It scans receipts in real-time, cross-references company policies, and alerts managers when something looks fishy - whether it's a $200 "team lunch" for one person or mysterious Uber rides during off-hours. You charge $15-30 per user per month, with enterprise add-ons for custom policy engines and integration packages.

The wedge is remote-first companies where traditional oversight falls apart. Your sales manager in Austin can't eyeball receipts from your developer in Denver. Expense Detective becomes the eyes and ears finance teams lost when everyone went remote. You start by targeting 50-500 person companies that moved remote during COVID and are still figuring out spend controls. Growth comes through finance leader LinkedIn groups, webinars about remote work fraud prevention, and partnerships with accounting firms who recommend you during client audits.

The beauty is in the behavioral layer - it learns what normal spending looks like for each role and location, then flags anomalies that would slip past rule-based systems. A $50 dinner receipt isn't suspicious unless it's from someone who typically spends $15 on lunch. You grow from expense oversight into a full financial intelligence platform, adding vendor analysis, budget forecasting, and procurement insights. Finance teams finally get the remote work visibility they've been missing, while employees get faster approvals and clearer spending guidelines.
